# Counterexample-Guided Maximal Elevation Protocol

This is an executable promotion layer over the existing Lean formalization. Lean remains authoritative for formal theorem claims.

## Seven mandatory gates

1. Exhaustive classification: all 6^6 = 46,656 deterministic operators on six states and three quotient classes.
2. Adversarial mutation: every single-output cross-class mutation plus a 2,000-case deterministic random campaign.
3. Metamorphic relabeling: 5,000 state-permutation cases.
4. Nested quotient closure: 16 -> 8 -> 4 tower, 10,000 generated candidates, 13 iterations.
5. Reconstruction: canonical section, 10,000 generated candidates.
6. Perturbation: deterministic perturbation family checked against an independently computed predicate.
7. Economic gate: real microbenchmark plus exact dense work ratio and end-to-end accounting.

Promotion is the conjunction of all seven gates. A failed gate is evidence and must become a regression fixture in future expansion.

Evidence classes:
- FORMAL: Lean-checked theorem.
- EXECUTABLE: exhaustive/generative finite verification.
- PERFORMANCE: environment-specific timing or exact operation-count model.
- HYPOTHESIS: anything not established by the preceding evidence.

Passing this gate does not establish universal speedup, optimizer dominance, quantum advantage, or any claim outside the tested construction.

The harness is stdlib-only, deterministic under seed 0xC0FFEE, and emits a SHA-256 receipt.
